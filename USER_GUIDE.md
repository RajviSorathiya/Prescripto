## For Patients

### Getting Started

#### Creating a New Account

1. Visit the Prescripto homepage.
2. Click on the "Register" button.
3. Fill in your personal details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Click "Create Account".
5. You'll receive a confirmation message once your account is created.

#### Logging In

1. Visit the Prescripto homepage.
2. Click on the "Login" button.
3. Enter your registered email and password.
4. Click "Login".

### Finding a Doctor

#### Browsing All Doctors

1. From your dashboard, click on "Find a Doctor".
2. Browse through the list of available doctors.
3. Use filters to narrow down your search:
   - Speciality
   - Availability
   - Ratings

#### Doctor Details

1. Click on a doctor's profile to view detailed information:
   - Professional background
   - Specialization
   - Experience
   - Fees
   - Available slots
   - Patient reviews

### Booking an Appointment

#### Selecting a Time Slot

1. From the doctor's profile, click "Book Appointment".
2. Select your preferred date from the calendar.
3. Choose an available time slot.
4. Review the appointment details:
   - Doctor's name
   - Date and time
   - Consultation fees

#### Making Payment

1. Choose your payment method:
   - Online payment (credit/debit card)
   - Cash payment (to be paid at the clinic)
2. If selecting online payment:
   - Enter your card details
   - Complete the payment process
3. Receive a confirmation message and appointment details.

### Managing Your Appointments

#### Viewing Your Appointments

1. Navigate to "My Appointments" from your dashboard.
2. View a list of all your appointments, sorted by date.
3. Each appointment card displays:
   - Doctor's name
   - Date and time
   - Status (upcoming, completed, cancelled)

#### Cancelling an Appointment

1. From "My Appointments", find the appointment you wish to cancel.
2. Click the "Cancel" button.
3. Confirm your decision to cancel the appointment.
4. If applicable, information about the refund process will be displayed.

### Managing Your Profile

#### Updating Personal Information

1. Click on your profile icon in the top right corner.
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu.
3. Update your personal details:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Address
   - Gender
   - Date of birth
4. Click "Save Changes".

#### Uploading a Profile Picture

1. From your profile page, click on the profile picture placeholder.
2. Select "Upload New Image".
3. Choose an image file from your device.
4. Crop or adjust the image if needed.
5. Click "Save".

## For Doctors

### Logging In as a Doctor

1. Navigate to the doctor login page.
2. Enter your email and password provided by the administrator.
3. Click "Login".
4. Upon first login, you may be prompted to change your password.

### Managing Your Dashboard

#### Understanding Your Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of your practice:
- Today's appointments
- Upcoming appointments
- Completed appointments
- Recent patient reviews

#### Viewing Statistics

Your dashboard includes key statistics:
- Total number of appointments
- Completion rate
- Cancellation rate
- Average rating

### Handling Appointments

#### Viewing All Appointments

1. Click on "Appointments" in the sidebar.
2. View a list of all your appointments.
3. Filter appointments by:
   - All
   - Today
   - Upcoming
   - Completed

#### Managing an Appointment

1. From the appointments list, click on a specific appointment.
2. View patient details and appointment information.
3. For upcoming appointments, you can:
   - Mark as completed (after the consultation)
   - Cancel (if necessary)
4. Add notes about the appointment if needed.

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click on your name in the sidebar.
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown.
3. Update your professional information:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Speciality
   - Experience
   - About me
   - Profile picture
4. Click "Save Changes".

### Setting Availability

1. Navigate to "Availability" in the sidebar.
2. Set your availability for each day of the week.
3. Specify working hours:
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Break times
4. Set consultation duration (e.g., 15, 30, 45 minutes).
5. Mark days off when you're not available.
6. Click "Save Schedule".

## For Administrators

### Admin Dashboard

#### Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the platform:
- Total number of doctors
- Total number of patients
- Total appointments
- Recent activities
- System statistics

#### Monitoring Activity

1. View real-time statistics on the dashboard.
2. Monitor booking trends.
3. Track user growth.
4. Review system performance metrics.

### Managing Doctors

#### Adding a New Doctor

1. Click on "Doctors" in the sidebar.
2. Select "Add New Doctor".
3. Fill in the doctor's information:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone number
   - Speciality
   - Experience
   - Fees
   - Profile picture
4. Set initial password.
5. Click "Add Doctor".
6. The doctor will receive login credentials via email.

#### Editing Doctor Information

1. Navigate to "Doctors" in the sidebar.
2. Find the doctor you wish to edit.
3. Click the "Edit" button.
4. Update the necessary information.
5. Click "Save Changes".

#### Removing a Doctor

1. Go to "Doctors" in the sidebar.
2. Find the doctor you wish to remove.
3. Click the "Remove" button.
4. Confirm the removal.
5. The doctor will be deactivated from the system.

### Overseeing Appointments

#### Viewing All Appointments

1. Click on "Appointments" in the sidebar.
2. View a complete list of all appointments in the system.
3. Filter appointments by:
   - Doctor
   - Status
   - Date range

#### Managing Appointment Issues

1. Identify problematic appointments from the list.
2. Click on an appointment to view details.
3. Take appropriate action:
   - Contact the doctor
   - Contact the patient
   - Cancel the appointment if necessary
   - Reschedule the appointment

### System Management

#### User Management

1. Navigate to "Users" in the sidebar.
2. View a list of all registered patients.
3. Search for specific users.
4. View user details.
5. Deactivate users if required.

#### System Settings

1. Click on "Settings" in the sidebar.
2. Manage system-wide settings:
   - Payment options
   - Email notifications
   - System maintenance
   - Security settings

## Common Tasks

### Logging In and Out

#### Logging In

1. Visit the Prescripto login page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Select your user type (Patient, Doctor, or Admin).
4. Click "Login".

#### Logging Out

1. Click on your profile icon in the top right corner.
2. Select "Logout" from the dropdown menu.
3. You will be redirected to the login page.

### Forgotten Password

1. On the login page, click "Forgot Password?".
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Click "Reset Password".
4. Check your email for a password reset link.
5. Click the link and follow the instructions to create a new password.

### Updating Profile Picture

1. Navigate to your profile settings.
2. Click on your current profile picture or the placeholder.
3. Select "Upload New Image".
4. Choose an image file from your device.
5. Crop or adjust the image if needed.
6. Click "Save".

### Notifications

#### Setting Up Notifications

1. Go to your profile settings.
2. Click on "Notifications".
3. Choose your notification preferences:
   - Email notifications
   - Browser notifications
   - Appointment reminders
4. Click "Save Preferences".

#### Viewing Notifications

1. Click on the bell icon in the navigation bar.
2. View all your recent notifications.
3. Click on a notification to see details.
4. Mark notifications as read by clicking the checkmark icon.

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Problem**: Unable to log in with correct credentials.

**Solution**:
1. Ensure you're using the correct email address.
2. Check if Caps Lock is on.
3. Try resetting your password.
4. Clear browser cookies and cache.

### Appointment Booking Errors

**Problem**: Unable to book an appointment.

**Solution**:
1. Ensure you're logged in.
2. Check if the time slot is still available.
3. Verify your payment method is valid.
4. Try selecting a different time slot.
5. Contact support if the issue persists.

### Profile Update Issues

**Problem**: Changes to profile not saving.

**Solution**:
1. Ensure all required fields are filled.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Ensure image files are in the correct format (JPG, PNG) and size (less than 5MB).
4. Try using a different browser.

### Technical Support

For any technical issues not covered in this guide:

1. Click on "Help" in the footer.
2. Select "Contact Support".
3. Fill in the support request form with details of your issue.
4. Include screenshots if possible.
5. Submit the form.
6. Our support team will respond within 24 hours.
